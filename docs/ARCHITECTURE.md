## Section order inside `src/app.js` (oldest first; later sections wrap earlier functions)
1. **Core.** Dates (`DAY`, `iso`, `parse`, `addDays`, `fmt`), the clock (`today()`, `appNow`, `appNowIso`, `stampText` near the top), seed data, the schedule engine, the Gantt, estimates, invoices, POs, bills, payments, bank, job cost, `render()`, the views.
2. **Accounting module.** Ledger (`ledger`, `glSync`, `glDerive`), COA (`GL_COA`), periods and locks, close, WIP, statements, Pack, Flash, report builder (`REP_DS`, `REP_DEFS`).
3. **IA layer.** `IA_GROUPS` (nav groups and their views), `ROLE_VIEWS`, overviews (`OV_BUILD`, `OV_TILES`, `renderOvPage`), tiles (`TL_SETS`, `TL_HOST`), `needs(gid)`.
4. **Assistant.**
   - Per-user assistant: `pa*`, store `DB.asst[uid]`.
   - Google layer:
     - `gc*`: OAuth through GIS; the token in memory and sessionStorage only.
     - `gs*`: Gmail/Calendar/Drive pulls.
     - `ga*`: drafts, holds, filing.
   - Query engine: `EQ_TABLES`, `erpQuery`, `erpResolve`.
   - Translators: `qa*`.
   - Model loop: `mind*` (tools only; results wrapped `{untrusted, source, data}`; writes become proposals).
5. **Round 1–2 lanes.** frontdoor, records, create, finance, ops, flow, integ, overview, locks, finance2, thread, ask, integ2.
6. **Round 4.**

   | Wave | Lanes, with each lane's function prefix |
   |---|---|
   | A | close (`fin3*`), payables (`pay3*`), onecount (`readyToBill`, `salesRows`, `jobNeeds`, `OC_BADGE`), ask4 (`a4*`, `ASK_INTENTS`), cause (`cz*`) |
   | B | billing (`bl*`, `billingNeeds`, `apOpenBills`), records2 (`rc*`), client (`cl*`), screens (`os*`, `t2*`), trust (`tr*`, `tcase`, `appWeek`, the clock rewrite) |
   | C | budget (`bud*`, `bva*`), bankfeed (`bf*`), submittals (`sm*`), field (`prg*`, `pst*`, `dl*`, `fx*`) |
   | Then | integ4, integ5 |
7. **Round 5.** askgen (`ag*`, `eqRows`, `eqSchema`), after the predicate registry: the schema-aware Ask parser and the one privacy scope under `erpQuery`.

## Overhead and true profitability (one model; `ohMonth` and friends, after `monthRange`)
- **The pool** for a month is what the P&L calls overhead that month: every operating-expense line of `companyPL(R)` (office payroll, Unallocated Labor 7005, rent 7350, insurance, software, vehicle, marketing…), less "Needs Category" (a bank line nobody has coded is not overhead until it is coded). Overhead bank lines post to their accounts through `GL_CAT_ACCT`; a PO with no job whose lines are linked to an overhead category posts its bill there (`poCat`).
- **The basis** is one setting, `DB.company.ohBasis` (Settings › Overhead Allocation…, audited): `cost` total direct cost (default), `labor` direct labor dollars, `hours` direct labor hours, `revenue` revenue earned. `OH_BASES` names them; `ohMethodText()` is the one sentence every surface prints.
- **Allocation** is month by month: `ohMonth(ym)` = {pool, lines, base per job, alloc per job (shares add to the pool to the cent), rate, uncoded}; `ohAlloc(range)` sums the months. A job's overhead to date is the sum of its months.
- **Burden rate** `ohTrailing()`: the last three full months' pool over their base; it prices a line's overhead at completion (`ohLineBurden`). `ohAbsorption` gives this month's under- or over-absorbed overhead (pool less rate × base). `ohNow()` gives the rate this month and year to date.
- **Profit**: `jobTrueProfit(p, range)` = earned revenue, job cost, gross profit, overhead allocated, net profit, method. `projectProfit` carries the same figures. Surfaces: the job report's Profit After Overhead (`.oh-job`), Portfolio and Pack (`table.oh-jobs`, `.oh-pack` rate line), Weekly Flash (`.oh-flash`), the overhead card (`ohCardHtml`), Ask (`ohAsk`: margin on a job, overhead in a month, profit after overhead, overhead rate). `tests/chain_test.py` step 8 recomputes all of it from the raw records.

## Purchases billed back to the client in job cost (`txnRebilled`, `jobRebill`, `billRebillPart`, before `jobFin`)
A reimbursable (a bank or card line marked billable or stamped with its invoice line, or a bill line on a PO line marked billed back) is cost of its own: it is in actual and the estimate at completion on the cost row `RB` ("Billed back to the client"), never on an estimate line; it is out of the cost-to-cost % complete; its invoices (`inv.rebill`) are revenue earned when billed. `jobFinDerive` holds the arithmetic: pct = (actual − rebill cost) ÷ (EAC − rebill cost), earned = contract × pct + rebill billed. The root `jobFin` now returns through `jobFinDerive` too.

  The due date is the day before the room's first task (today when it is under way) or an order's need-by.
- `xsGate(row)`: a revision in an open conflict is issued but stays In Revision (`xsGate {ids}`) until the conflicts resolve. The Issue a Set dialog refuses a set issued "for construction" with a conflict in it.
- `xsResolve(fid, {stand: a|b, value, changed, payer, amount, days, issuer})`: issues the next revision of the sheet that changes, with the standing value, through `plIssue`. The hold's `liftOn` becomes that revision (lifted by `plAck` once everyone acknowledges; `plAck`'s wrapper restores the stages the hold changed). Then `xsMoney`: our cost, a draft change order (`newDoc`), a drafted backcharge notice, or the sub's cost, plus the ripple (orders to correct, schedule days). `xsDismiss(fid, why)` judges a finding not a conflict and lifts its holds.
- `xsMeasures(pid)`: found, before, field, open, avoided (the `reworkEst` of findings found before work), ours, backcharge, co, avgDays, pctBefore. `xsPatterns()` counts by set pair, hand-off and who was asked. `xsChecklist()` turns the hand-offs into the pre-issue checklist.
