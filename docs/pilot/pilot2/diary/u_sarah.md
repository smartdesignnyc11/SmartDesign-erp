A designer's day is mostly answering: Angel, the client, LPC, the architect. The app is good at taking an answer in and saying it went: the Answer sheet, Make It a Task, Log. It is weak at closing the loop. An answered field question stays open as a task. An answered client decision stays open as a decision. A client's mail never meets the decision it answers. So my evening is a reconciliation job that the record should have done itself. The re-issue list is the best thing on the page for me: one dimension change and six sheets named. I would not get that from Procore without building it by hand. What slowed me most is that decisions live in three counts (11 on the tile, 6 cards, 8 in Projects > Decisions) and all six cards on Today read "no date". Those decisions, with their dates, are what my client conversations turn on, and here they are undated. I would change two things first. Make decisions dated and tappable from Today. Make an answer (in the Answer sheet, a mail or the portal) close the task or decision it answers, with a confirm.
