From Alan's screenshot of the circled strips on Vendors, Inventory, Controls, Ledger and Contacts: *is this the best information to have here, is it clean, where does it take me when I click it, how is the wording.*

## What the leaders do, and what we took
A research pass on the summary row of each module in NetSuite (the split between the **Reminders** portlet — exception counts, colour-banded, each a link into the filtered list — and the **KPI** portlet of totals with comparisons), Sage Intacct, QuickBooks (the **money bar**: the tile *is* the filter, with a visible Clear), Bill.com, Expensify, Procore (Project Overview's overdue / due in 7 / later bands; the commitments header), Buildertrend, Fieldwire, HubSpot (deal insights recompute from the list; Forecast's **Gap** and **Coverage** as derived, decision-shaped numbers), Pipedrive, Xero, Ramp, and the generic craft (Stripe/Linear stat tiles, empty states).

The rules the strip now follows: a tile is a question with a number and an action; exceptions before totals; today and this week before all-time; the tile is the filter — tap and the list narrows to exactly the rows behind the number, with *Showing · Clear*; three tones only (neutral, amber, red) and colour means state, never magnitude; labels in Title Case, three words or fewer, naming a state not an object (*Late to Site*, not *Purchase Orders*); a context line in lowercase, under sixty characters, that gives the cause, the consequence or the comparison and never restates the label; zero states say the good news specifically (*every vendor current on insurance and license*); four tiles; no vanity metrics. Three deliberate departures: zero-count tiles stay (NetSuite hides them; a strip that changes shape is unlearnable), and when every tile is zero the whole strip collapses to one *All clear* line; vendor compliance is its own tile (QuickBooks buries it); green is not a state.
