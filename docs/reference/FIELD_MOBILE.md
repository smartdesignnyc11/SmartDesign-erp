# Field mobile pass — the foreman, the driver and the crew on a phone (2026-09-13)

Module `erp/field_mobile.js` · CSS in `ia.css` (the `fm-*` rules and the crewmode block) · tests `erp/field_mobile_test.py` (41 checks at 390×844) plus the earlier foreman suites. Artifact v78. Builds on the office pass (`mobile.js`): bottom sheets, 16px inputs, card tables, tel/mailto links and the offline banner already applied to these roles; this pass is what is specific to a person on site.

## Where it came from
The same research brief (Procore Quick Capture, Raken's daily log, Fieldwire, CompanyCam, Buildertrend's two-thing offline, Workyard's supervisor clock-in, Jobber's home and context-sensitive +), then an audit of the fifteen field screens, a build, and a field-product reviewer who walked the three roles with Playwright and came back with eleven defects and a tap count for every common job.

## What was wrong
The foreman's screens were strong (Today, Say it, the pre-filled log — the reviewer's own words: "beats Raken"). The crew and driver were living in the office's screens: the Clock tab carried *Add time entry* and *Build payroll* and a 200-word brief; Schedule was the full Gantt with track filters and Print; Documents was a desktop dropzone with six type filters; Chat asked for your name and put the composer mid-page with an emoji clip; the driver's route had no way to navigate and no way to call the site; the office sub-tabs sat above the field tab bar saying the same things twice. Buttons were 36px, small text 11–12px, and the Say it button covered Approve and Fix on the very row that needed a decision.

## What is there now
**Crew — Clock is home.** Clock in/out, then *Today*: the tasks on the schedule with your name (or "Crew"), each with **Need something** that opens Requests with the task already in the draft; your open requests with their state (sent · acknowledged · ordered · on the truck). No office buttons, no brief paragraph, no sub-tabs. **Schedule is My week**: five site days, today marked, what is yours per day, *also on site that day* folded, and *Show the whole schedule* for the rare time it is wanted. **Photos** is camera-first: one 64px **Take a photo** (rear camera, several at once, compressed on the phone), **Delivery ticket** and **Receipt** beside it; the sheet that follows asks which task and one line, and the file lands on the project with your name, the time and the task, and a line in the log. **Chat** knows who you are — no name box — the composer is fixed at the thumb above the tabs with a 44px field and a line-icon camera, and the keyboard's key says Send.

**Driver — the Route is a run sheet.** *Up next* is the first stop, highlighted, with **Navigate** (Apple Maps to the store's coordinates); site stops carry **Call Angel**; **Clock in** sits in the Route header so the day starts without changing tabs; the van gauge moved under the stop instead of beside it. **Receipts**: **Snap a receipt** opens the camera and drops the photo straight into the Spend reader — vendor, job, date, *Read the receipt*, lines with cost codes, EN/ES — so a receipt becomes a cost, not a photo. Delivery ticket and Photo beside it.

**Foreman.** **Clock the crew** on the Crew tab: one sheet, *Everyone is here* or tick by name, in and out, each entry marked *clocked in by Angel* with his location, audited, logged. The *N things missing for today's work* line opens the full list with **Raise it**. **Say it** now docks as a bar above the tabs, so it never sits on a button, and steps aside while a sheet is open. The parser no longer files "the insulation is not here" as a sub no-show — a missing material is a need to the coordinator; a missing crew is a no-show to the PM. Project chat is in the menu. The sub button says **Confirm**. Sub status prints as words, not the enum.

**Everywhere on site.** 44px buttons and controls, 24px checkboxes, a 13px floor, status pills that stay pill-sized, the title bar says what the tab says (Clock · Photos · Receipts · Route), and the office sub-tabs are gone from field roles.

## The check
Reviewer's findings and what happened: Say it over Approve/Fix (docked); "not here" → no-show (parser guard); foreman could not reach chat (menu; Requests deliberately stays behind Ask, per the earlier foreman pass); Clock-the-crew rows broken (fixed, plus Tick all); counts jammed into words (my own flex rule — removed); tall status pills (align-self); raw `noshow` (mapped); three names for one screen (title from the tab); 32px inputs, 13px checkboxes, 36px chips (44/24/40); "Confirmed" as a verb (Confirm). Not done, in order of value: the material-need form asks for the cost code before the need (the office should code it); a map on the route; photo markup and a gallery by room; plan sheets with pins; a safety incident form behind the parser's `incident`; per-project offline beyond the local store.

Tap counts now: clock in 1 (all three roles, driver from the Route); raise a need by voice 3; crew need 2 (Need something → say it); confirm a sub 2; send the log 2; snap a receipt 3 plus the read; see today 0 (foreman) / 0 (crew, on the clock screen); message the office 2.

Checks: `field_mobile_test.py` 41/41, `mobile_test.py` 51/51, the full regression (89 suites) clean, hosted app 28/28.
