# Mobile pass — the office on a phone (2026-09-13)

Module `erp/mobile.js` · CSS in `ia.css` (`.mtabs`, `.m-*`, the 760px block) · tests `erp/mobile_test.py` (51 checks at 390×844, plus desktop no-change checks). Artifact v77. Field roles (foreman, driver, crew) keep crewmode and their own tab bars; this layer is for everyone who runs the company from a phone between sites — owner, PM, coordinator, billing, controller, design director, designer.

## Where it came from
A research brief on the mobile leaders before a line was written: Procore (Quick Capture — record many, review once; role-based home as a work queue), Fieldwire (thumb-anchored tools, per-project offline), Raken (daily log with a + per section, saved signature), CompanyCam (photo as the record; project QR), Buildertrend (offline scoped to two things), ServiceTitan (present options on the device), Jobber (five bottom tabs, Home = today + Up Next, context-sensitive +), HubSpot mobile (caller ID, **the post-call prompt**, card scanner), Salesforce (Briefcase offline, Quick Actions as big buttons), NetSuite mobile (approvals, receipt capture, KPI scorecards), QuickBooks (the explicit mobile/web split — billing does not move to the phone), Expensify (SmartScan auto-match), Slack/Linear (25-second sessions, swipe to act). Rules: 44pt targets with 8pt gaps, primary actions in the bottom 40%, bottom sheets not centred modals, `inputmode`/`enterkeyhint`/`autocomplete`, 16px inputs so iOS never zooms, `capture="environment"`, `viewport-fit=cover` + safe-area insets, `100dvh`, table→card, no hamburger for primary navigation (hidden items get half the use). Then an audit of 27 screens at iPhone size, a build, and a power-user review that walked five roles and 36 views with Playwright.

## What was wrong
Office roles had no bottom navigation — a hamburger and a horizontally clipped tab strip. Eleven screens scrolled sideways (data tables at 750–1,280px; Billing's card at 902px; Reports' entity bar; the Sales Workspace grid). Every wide table was a desktop grid pinched onto a phone. Modals opened centred with Save at the top; the editor drawer's Save sat under the tab bar once tabs existed. Inputs were 13px, so iOS zoomed on every tap. No phone number anywhere was dialable. Nothing could be captured in fewer than four taps. The rail's Settings button was white-on-white (desktop too).

## What is there now
**Bottom tabs by role** (`#mtabs`, 52px, safe-area aware). Owner: Brief · Today · Approve · Sales · More. PM: Brief · Today · Field · Approve. Coordinator: Brief · Today · Requests · Buy. Billing: Brief · Billing · Approve · Bills. Controller: … · Banking. Design director: My Day · Studio · Drawings · Approve. Designer: My Day · Board · Drawings · Filings. **More** opens the full menu (44px rows, 13px floor). The Approve tab carries the count.

**Approvals** (`view='mapprove'`, also a Home tab on desktop): every yes/no waiting on this person as a card with the three facts and 44px **Approve / Reject / Open** — vendor bills (PM *Recommend* first when the amount needs the Owner; the reason is printed), hours submitted (never your own), questions from the site addressed to you, decisions we owe, field requests not yet acknowledged, then the rest of *Needs me*. The same rules fire as on the desktop: a vendor with a bank change pending cannot be approved (the card says so and Open goes to Vendors); separation of duties; control flags listed; compliance blocks named on the card, and approving anyway asks the Owner for the override reason in a sheet rather than a `prompt()` — the reason lands in the bill's approvals trail. Approve, stay on the list, next.

**Tables become cards.** Any grid with four or more columns turns into one card per row on the phone: the first cell is the title, every other cell is *label · value* (labels come from the header, sort glyphs stripped). Cards keep what matters — money, a status pill, the first date, any button — and fold the rest behind **+ N more** (tap to open; the row's own tap still opens the record). Editable line tables and the takeoff grid stay grids inside their own horizontal scroller. No screen scrolls the page sideways.

**Bottom sheets.** Every modal is a sheet from the bottom with a grab handle, a scrolling body, and a sticky footer whose buttons are 46px and clear the home bar; the editor drawer sits above the tabs with the same footer. The Sales panels and composers go full-width. Inputs are 16px, 44px tall; number fields open the decimal keypad; search boxes get the Search key; date fields get Done.

**Capture** (`#fab`, the +): a sheet with Photo to the project (rear camera, a one-line note, saved to Documents and the log), Receipt (the Spend reader), Note to the project log, Task (for whom, due, priority), Decision needed (who decides, by when, what it holds), Log a call (on a deal — a touch on the lead — or a project), Ask the assistant. The + steps aside whenever a sheet, drawer, menu or the assistant is open, so it never covers a button.

**Calls.** Phone numbers and emails on any screen are `tel:` and `mailto:` links. When you come back to the app after tapping a number, a bar offers **Log the call with Priya?** — one tap opens the call form on the deal you were on (HubSpot's post-call prompt). The driver's stops carry **Navigate** (Apple Maps to the store's coordinates).

**Navigation details.** The active sub-tab scrolls into view; when more tabs sit to the right the strip fades so you know. Filter pills on the Sales index are one swipeable row; deals open as a list on the phone and the board swipes one stage at a time (`scroll-snap`). Segmented controls wrap instead of clipping. Page headers stack and align left (the driver's *Route* no longer hugs the right edge). The Brief's four home cards are single-column. An iOS Safari user sees, once, how to put the app on the home screen (`apple-mobile-web-app-*` meta, `viewport-fit=cover`, theme colour); an offline banner says what is true — everything is saved on the phone and syncs when the signal is back.

## The check
The review found: the editor drawer's Save under the tab bar (fixed: drawer above tabs, footer clears the home bar); the FAB covering Reject and amounts (fixed: 150px of room at the bottom, FAB hides while anything is open, smaller); phones not dialable (fixed everywhere); cards ten rows tall (fixed: fold to the facts that matter); 30px menu rows, 26px entity toggle, 13px checkboxes (fixed: 44/40/22); 11px rail text (13px floor); the takeoff header wrapping in four columns (card headers wrap); the project chip truncating its own name (wider); Recommend vs Approve unexplained (the card explains); *Open* as bare text (a bordered button); the driver's stops with no map (Navigate); a blank white box in the menu (the Settings button, now legible); no sign of the clipped tab strip (the fade). Still open, in order of value: swipe-to-approve and pull-to-refresh; the Brief's named items as links; a map on the route; per-project "take this job offline" beyond the local store; push notifications (needs the installed PWA on iOS).

Checks: `mobile_test.py` 51/51; the full regression (87 suites) clean; hosted app 28/28.
