# The core journeys (Alan, 10-26)

Progress is measured by these journeys and nothing else: **N of 10 pass**, on the phone and on the desktop, through the screens.

A journey passes when it meets all three conditions:
1. **The screen-only walk** (`tests/cycle_walk.py`: no AI, menu by label, tap, type, pick; never a function call or a view jump) does it from a blank start, and the referee (`sim/cycle.py`) finds the records right: the fields, the links and the money.
2. **It stays inside its budget:** taps and minutes, on that device.
3. **The role expert walks it** in the improvement loop (`docs/IMPROVEMENT_LOOP.md`) and grades it 9 or higher. A 9 means a 20-year professional in that seat would choose it over the tool they use today, with no severity-1 or severity-2 finding left.

Every journey starts new: a new client, job or vendor, not the seeded ones. Each one names what a professional checks before calling it done, and the Standard measure it serves.

**Every journey also checks the start of the day.** The person's Today shows what they owe, in one place, with the promised time. That covers mail, calls and tasks. The 2-hour client rule and the Standard's measures are checked by the app, not by memory.

**How a journey is added or changed:** Alan's call, written here with the date. The walk (`tests/cycle_walk.py`) and the referee (`sim/cycle.py`) get the matching step check in the same commit.
